Frequently asked questions

Is my data separate from other firms?

Yes. Each firm's data is completely isolated at the database level. You cannot see other firms' data, and they cannot see yours. This is enforced by row-level security policies.

Which currencies do you support?

ReportNoReport supports Malaysian Ringgit (MYR), Singapore Dollar (SGD), and US Dollar (USD). You choose one currency per firm when you sign up, and it applies to all ledgers and statements.

Do I need to pay anything right now?

No. ReportNoReport is free while we're in early access, with full access to every feature. There's nothing to enter and nothing to cancel. If that changes in future, we'll tell existing firms well in advance.

Can I import from a spreadsheet?

Yes. You can import clients and transactions via CSV during initial setup and at any time afterward. ReportNoReport shows you the import format and validates your data before committing.

Who can see what?

You control access completely. Assign roles to your team: owner or admin (full access), accountant (posts transactions), or viewer (read-only). Each role has finely-tuned permissions.
