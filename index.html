Frequently asked questions

Is my data separate from other firms?

Yes. Each firm's data is completely isolated. You cannot see other firms' data, and they cannot see yours.

Which currencies do you support?

Platform supports Malaysian Ringgit (MYR), Singapore Dollar (SGD), and US Dollar (USD). You choose one currency per firm.

What happens after 14 days?

Your account becomes read-only until you pay. You can still download and export all your data, so you're never locked in.

Can I import from a spreadsheet?

Yes. You can import clients and transactions via CSV during setup and at any time.

Who can see what?

You control it. Assign roles to your team: Admin (full access), Manager (all clients), or Client (view own account only).
